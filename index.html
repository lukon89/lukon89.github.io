Who I am

A bit about my background and what drives me.

I'm a senior Python backend engineer with a background in fintech, currently working at OakNorth — a UK-based digital bank — where I build and maintain event-driven microservices, REST APIs, and data pipelines. My day-to-day work involves Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, AWS, and Terraform, with a focus on clean architecture, reliability, and developer experience.
